FAQ

Common questions

I can't log in, what do I do?

Check you're using the login details given to you by the office. If you've forgotten your password or your account isn't working, contact support and we'll sort it out.

How do I request materials as a builder?

Open the app, go to Requests, and add the items and quantities you need. The office gets notified and will action it from their end.

Why can't I see certain stock or locations?

Access is set up per staff member. If something's missing that you need for your role, ask the office to update your permissions.

The app isn't loading or is stuck, what now?

Try closing and reopening the app first. If that doesn't fix it, check your internet connection. Still stuck? Email support with your device and what you were doing when it happened.

Can I use this app if I don't work for the business?

No. Next Logistics is built for internal use by this business only, it isn't a public inventory tool.
