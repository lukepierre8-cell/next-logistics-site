Next Logistics Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 11, 2026

Next Logistics ("we", "us") is a stock and materials management app for construction businesses, operated from the Cook Islands. This policy explains what information the app collects, why, and the choices you have. Questions? Email nextlogisticssupport@gmail.com.

Information we collect

  • Account information: your display name, password, role (office staff or builder), trade, and the company you create or join (company name and join code). We do not ask for your email address or phone number. Passwords are handled by our authentication provider and are not stored in readable form.
  • Business data you enter: products, stock levels, costs, suppliers, storage locations, stock movements, builder requests, shipments and notes.
  • Files you upload: spreadsheets, PDFs and images you choose to import (for example supplier price lists or stock sheets).
  • Photos: only if a builder attaches photos to a stock request. The app asks for camera or photo access at that moment.
  • Location: only if you tap the map picker to set a build location. The app asks for permission first and uses your location only while you are using that feature. You can also drop a pin by hand without sharing your location.
  • Optional Microsoft account access: if office staff choose to connect OneDrive or SharePoint Excel files, the app reads those workbooks through Microsoft with your permission.

How we use it

  • To create and manage your account and show you your company's data.
  • To track stock, process builder requests, and show shipments.
  • To read and clean up spreadsheets you import, including using an AI service to help structure the rows.
  • To send notifications on your device (these are generated on your device by the app).
  • To keep the app secure and working.

We do not sell your information. We do not show ads. We do not track you across other apps or websites.

Who can see your data

  • Your company: data is separated by company. Only people who sign in with your company's join code can see your company's data. Builders do not see pricing.
  • Service providers that run the app for us:
    • Supabase, which hosts our database, file storage and sign-in.
    • An AI service, which receives spreadsheet content you import so it can help structure product rows.
    • Microsoft, only if you choose to connect OneDrive or SharePoint.
    They only process data to provide the app and are not allowed to use it for their own advertising.
  • Legal reasons: we may share information if required by law.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Your sign-in session is kept in secure storage on your device. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong password and do not share your company join code with people outside your business.

How long we keep it

We keep your account and company data while your account is active. If you ask us to delete your account, we delete it as described below.

Your choices and deleting your data

You can ask us to delete your account and the personal information linked to it at any time. See how to delete your account. You can also turn off camera, photo, location or notification permissions in your phone's settings at any time.

Children

Next Logistics is a business tool and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top. Using the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.
