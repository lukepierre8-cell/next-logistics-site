Delete your Next Logistics account

You can ask us to delete your Next Logistics account and the personal information linked to it.

How to request deletion

  1. Email nextlogisticssupport@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my Next Logistics account".
  2. Include the display name on your account and your company name or join code, so we can find the right account.
  3. We may reply to confirm the request is really from you.

What happens next

  • We delete your account within 30 days of confirming your request.
  • Deleted: your sign-in, display name, role and trade, and any photos you attached to requests.
  • May be kept: stock records, requests and movement history that belong to your company, because they are your employer's business records. They will no longer be linked to your name where we can reasonably remove it.
  • If you are the only member of a company and want everything removed, say so in your email and we will delete the whole company's data.

See our Privacy Policy for more.
